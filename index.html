Our story

A platform for artists the world overlooks

At Vibusha, we give a stand to artists who truly deserve appreciation but face constraints in reaching the world. We work with tribes across India — and beyond — whose art and heritage are endangered, giving them a platform to showcase their culture in whatever form speaks to them.

We don't burden our artisans with quantity. Each artist decides how much they create and at what pace — no mass production, no two pieces identical. A fixed share of every order goes directly back to the artisan behind it.

"Each brush stroke carries a story that deserves to be seen."
  • Supporting artisans from endangered and lesser-known tribes, in India and across the world
  • No mass production — every artist sets their own pace and quantity
  • A fixed portion of every order goes directly back to the artisan
  • Every piece is one of a kind, shaped by the artist's own hands
  • Every resin order supports our stray animal care initiative

FAQ

Good to know

How long does delivery take?

Local Dubai delivery typically takes 2–4 days once your order is confirmed. Express delivery is also available — ask us when you order.

Can I customize my order?

Yes — message us with what you have in mind. Cover motifs, inscriptions, page layouts, colours, and embedded elements are all possible depending on the product.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes. Reach out via WhatsApp with your quantity, customization requests, and timeline and we'll work out a plan together.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, card payments, and bank transfer — all supported. Details confirmed once your order is placed.

How does Vibusha support artisans and stray animals?

A fixed portion of every stationery order goes directly to the artisan who made it. Every resin order contributes to our stray animal care initiative — feeding, vaccination, and care for the wounded.
